over the next couple of years, oai, anthropic, meta, google are going to consistently build more and more integrations with tools that we consistently use until they fully abstract away everyone's interaction with the internet. why edit photos or videos manually if they can do it for you? why create presentations, economic models, or documentation if they can create them for you? all of your personal and business information will be able to be viewed and queried at any time through one interface.

humans love abstracting away problems. it is one of our greatest gifts. you don't have to understand physics, hardware or software to use an iphone. you don't have to understand an internal combustion engine or a battery to drive a car. but just as those abstractions freed us, they also fundamentally changed what we, as humans, are capable of. and more importantly, what we feel we are capable of. think of the common parent-to-kid teaching: showing their kid how to fix a flat tire or change the oil in a car. i would venture to guess more than 75% of the people i know don't know how to do either

once we abstract away problems, we often lose the joy of learning and miss the pride of understanding—the belief that we are competent and independent.

these abstractions give rise to industries of people who understand: in the above examples, physicists, engineers, and mechanics who understand what others don't need to. some people will care that they don't understand, and some people won't. either is okay.

but, i care.
